Can I Afford the Life I Want?

Use the pay you found and the rent in the city where you want to live. Look up rent on a site like Zillow or Apartments.com (search "apartments in your city"). These numbers are rough guesses, and that is okay.

Take-home pay is estimated at about 75% of your pay (taxes take roughly 25%). The "30% rule" says rent should be no more than 30% of your pay before taxes.

Is this a good source?

Not everything online is true. Check each one before you trust a website.

Pay tips

  • Pay changes by city, experience, and employer. Write a range, like $45,000 to $65,000.
  • "Average" or "median" pay is the middle. Starting pay is usually lower.
  • Job posting sites show what employers offer right now. Government sites show the average for the whole country or state.
  • If two sites agree, you can trust the number more.

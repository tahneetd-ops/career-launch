💡 The Key Insight
Both people are right — for themselves. The goal of values work isn't to find the "correct" values. It's to understand YOUR values clearly enough that you can find careers that actually fit the life you want to live. A mismatch between your values and your career is one of the leading causes of job unhappiness — even in high-paying jobs. Self-awareness here is a genuine career superpower.

🔐 Last-Minute Intel Before You Enter
💬 Pause before you answer. 2–3 seconds of thinking looks confident, not unprepared.
👁️ Make eye contact — but you don't have to stare. Look at them when you're listening, glance away when you're thinking.
🖐️ Sit up straight, hands visible. Body language says as much as your words.
✅ If you don't know an answer, say "That's a great question — let me think about that for a moment." Never fake it.
🎯 End every answer by connecting back to why you'd be great for the role.

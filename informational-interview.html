🎙️
What Is It?
An informational interview is a conversation you request with someone who works in a career you're curious about. You're NOT asking for a job. You're asking for their story, knowledge, and advice.
🧠
Why Does It Matter?
It's one of the most powerful career tools available — and almost nobody your age uses it. A 20-minute conversation with a professional tells you more than hours of online research.
🤝
The Golden Rule
You are the interviewer. You set up the meeting, you ask the questions, you take notes, and you send a thank-you afterward. The professional is doing YOU a favor by giving their time.
📊
The Stats
Up to 70–80% of jobs are filled through networking — not job postings. Informational interviews are the first step in building that network. The earlier you start, the better.
📋 Informational Interview vs. Job Interview
🎙️ Informational Interview
→ YOU requested the meeting
→ YOU ask most of the questions
→ No job is being offered
→ Casual, low-pressure conversation
→ Goal: learn and build a connection
→ Typical length: 20–30 minutes
💼 Job Interview
→ THEY set up the meeting
→ THEY ask most of the questions
→ A specific job is on the line
→ Formal, higher stakes
→ Goal: get hired
→ Typical length: 30–60 minutes
💡 Real talk: Most professionals are happy to do informational interviews — especially with students. The worst they can say is "no" or "not right now." Asking is a soft skill called initiative — and it sets you apart immediately.
